# CAT 793 Mining Truck – Reliability, Maintainability & Availability (RMA) Analysis

This repository contains a Reliability, Maintainability, and Availability (RMA) study of the **Caterpillar 793 Mining Truck**, one of the most widely used off-highway haul trucks in large-scale surface mining operations. The project investigates the operational performance of the CAT 793, identifies critical failure modes of its major subsystems, and evaluates strategies to improve overall equipment effectiveness and reduce unplanned downtime.

## Project Overview

Heavy mining equipment such as the CAT 793 operates under severe duty cycles, extreme environmental conditions, and continuous load variations. These factors directly influence the reliability of subsystems like the engine, drivetrain, hydraulics, braking, tires, and electrical/electronic controls. This study uses field failure data and maintenance records to:

- Quantify reliability and availability of the truck and its sub-assemblies.
- - Identify the most failure-prone components and their dominant failure modes.
  - - Estimate Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF), Mean Time To Repair (MTTR), and overall availability.
    - - Fit suitable statistical distributions (e.g., Weibull, Exponential, Lognormal) to time-between-failure data.
      - - Recommend preventive and condition-based maintenance strategies to enhance fleet productivity.

            - ## Methodology
           
            - The analysis follows a standard RMA workflow:
           
            - 1. **Data Collection** – Compilation of failure and repair records for the CAT 793 fleet.
              2. 2. **System Decomposition** – Breakdown of the truck into major subsystems for component-level analysis.
                 3. 3. **Reliability Modeling** – Estimation of MTBF, failure rates, and reliability functions using statistical distribution fitting.
                    4. 4. **Maintainability Analysis** – Evaluation of MTTR and repair-time distributions.
                       5. 5. **Availability Assessment** – Calculation of inherent and operational availability for the truck and its subsystems.
                          6. 6. **Recommendations** – Suggested improvements in preventive maintenance scheduling and spare-parts management.
