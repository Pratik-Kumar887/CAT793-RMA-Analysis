# CAT 793 Mining Truck — Reliability, Maintainability & Availability (RMA) Analysis

This repository presents a Reliability, Maintainability, and Availability (RMA) study of the **Caterpillar 793 Mining Truck**, one of the most widely used off-highway haul trucks in large-scale surface mining operations. The work investigates the truck's operational performance, identifies critical failure modes of its major subsystems, and evaluates strategies to improve overall equipment effectiveness and reduce unplanned downtime.

## Project Overview

Heavy mining equipment such as the CAT 793 operates under severe duty cycles, extreme environmental conditions, and continuous load variations. These factors directly influence the reliability of subsystems such as the engine, drivetrain, hydraulics, braking, tires, and electrical/electronic controls. Using field failure and maintenance data, this study aims to:

- Quantify the reliability and availability of the truck and its sub-assemblies.
- Identify the most failure-prone components and their dominant failure modes.
- Estimate Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF), Mean Time To Repair (MTTR), and overall availability.
- Fit suitable statistical distributions (Weibull, Exponential, Lognormal) to time-between-failure data.
- Recommend preventive and condition-based maintenance strategies to enhance fleet productivity.

## Methodology

The analysis follows a standard RMA workflow:

1. **Data Collection** — Compile failure and repair records for the CAT 793 fleet.
2. **System Decomposition** — Break the truck down into major subsystems for component-level analysis.
3. **Reliability Modeling** — Estimate MTBF, failure rates, and reliability functions using statistical distribution fitting.
4. **Maintainability Analysis** — Evaluate MTTR and repair-time distributions.
5. **Availability Assessment** — Calculate inherent and operational availability for the truck and its subsystems.
6. **Recommendations** — Suggest improvements to preventive maintenance scheduling and spare-parts management.
